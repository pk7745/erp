# USER GUIDE - BMS COLLEGE ERP

Welcome to the modernized BMS College ERP System!

---

## 🔑 Logging In

1. Open your browser and navigate to the deployed ERP URL.
2. Enter your assigned username (e.g., `admin` or your faculty username).
3. Enter your secure password and click **Login**.

---

## 📊 Navigating the Portal

- **Dashboard**: View your assigned tasks, department metrics, and quick action shortcuts.
- **Timetable**: View weekly lecture schedules and room allocations.
- **Group Chat**: Communicate with college departments and faculty.
- **Attendance**: Mark daily student/staff attendance.
- **Leave Mgmt**: Apply for leave and view leave approval status.
- **Expenses**: Submit expense reimbursement claims.
- **My Profile**: View and update profile details, payslips, and documents.

---

## 🌙 Dark Mode Toggle

Click the **🌙 Toggle Dark Mode** button located at the bottom of the left sidebar to switch between light and dark themes anytime.
